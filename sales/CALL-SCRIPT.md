# Sales Call Script
### For prospects we know need Praxis

---

## Pre-Call Prep (2 min)

**Know before you dial:**
- [ ] What compliance framework? (CMMC, HIPAA, SOC2, etc.)
- [ ] Company size / team size
- [ ] Upcoming audit or deadline?
- [ ] Who's their current "expert everyone asks"?

---

## Opening (30 sec)

> "Hey [NAME], this is [YOU] from [COMPANY]. I know you're busy so I'll be quick — I'm reaching out because I understand you're working toward [CMMC Level 2 / HIPAA compliance / etc.], and I've got something that's going to save your team a ton of time. Got 3 minutes?"

**If yes:** Continue to Discovery  
**If no:** "When's better — tomorrow morning or Thursday afternoon?"

---

## Discovery (2 min)

Don't ask if they have the problem. **Confirm the details.**

> "So you're working toward [COMPLIANCE]. Quick question — when your team has a question about a control or requirement, who do they ask?"

*Wait for answer. They'll name a person or say "we look it up."*

> "Got it. And how much time would you say [THAT PERSON / your team] spends answering those kinds of questions? Per week, roughly."

*They'll give a number. Anchor it.*

> "Yeah, that's what we hear. Usually 5-10 hours a week for the senior person. And the frustrating part is it's often the same questions over and over, right?"

---

## Bridge (30 sec)

> "So here's why I'm calling. We built something that basically turns your compliance documentation into an AI expert — but not the kind that makes stuff up. It only answers from your docs, it cites sources, and here's the key: when it gets something wrong, you correct it, and it remembers forever.
>
> After a few weeks of your team using it, you've basically captured all that tribal knowledge that's currently stuck in [EXPERT'S] head."

---

## The Hook (30 sec)

> "We've got one running for CMMC right now — 2,000 controls indexed, gives you the PowerShell commands to pull evidence, knows what assessors actually look for. And it learns. Every time someone teaches it something, it gets smarter.
>
> I'd love to show you what it looks like. Takes 15 minutes, and you can throw your hardest compliance question at it."

---

## Close for Demo (30 sec)

> "What's easier for you — [DAY] morning or [DAY] afternoon?"

**If they hesitate:**
> "Look, worst case you see something cool and it's not for you right now. Best case, you find something that saves [EXPERT] 5 hours a week and captures knowledge before someone leaves. 15 minutes."

---

## Common Responses

### "Send me some info"
> "Absolutely, I'll shoot you a one-pager right after this. But honestly, it makes way more sense when you see it in action — can we do 15 minutes on [DAY]? I'll send the info now and you can review before we meet."

### "We're not ready yet"
> "Totally get it. When's the audit / deadline? ... Okay, so [X months] out. The reason I'd still want to show you now is the system gets smarter with use — teams that start 6 months early have captured 500+ refinements by audit time. The ones that start late are still training it during crunch time."

### "We already have a solution"
> "Oh nice, what are you using? ... [Listen] ... Got it. Does it learn from your team's corrections? Like, when your expert says 'actually, that's not quite right,' does it remember for next time? [They'll say no.] That's the difference. This one compounds."

### "What does it cost?"
> "Depends on scope, but for a pilot — 4 weeks to prove it out — we're talking [$X]. That gets credited to the full implementation if you move forward. But let's not get ahead of ourselves — let me show you what it does first, and if it's not obviously valuable, we won't waste time on pricing."

### "I need to talk to [SOMEONE ELSE]"
> "Makes sense. Let's do this — I'll show you the demo, you'll be able to explain it way better than any email I send, and then you can bring [THEM] into the next conversation. Sound good?"

## After the Call

- [ ] Send one-pager immediately
- [ ] Calendar invite for demo
- [ ] Add notes to CRM
- [ ] Follow-up task if no demo scheduled

---

## Demo Day Prep

Before the demo:
1. Ask them to bring 2-3 real questions they'd ask their expert
2. Have CMMC-Buddy (or their vertical) ready
3. Know their framework / deadline / pain points

During the demo:
1. Let THEM type the question
2. Show the answer, show the citations
3. Show the "Refine" button — explain the flywheel
4. Ask: "What would it mean for your team if this answered 80% of these questions correctly?"

Close:
> "So here's what I'd suggest — let's do a 4-week pilot. We pick one domain, get your team using it, measure accuracy. If it doesn't hit 85%, you walk away. If it does, we talk about full rollout. Sound fair?"

## Key Phrases to Use

- "It learns and never forgets"
- "Captures tribal knowledge before it walks out the door"
- "Not a chatbot that makes stuff up — only answers from YOUR docs"
- "The system gets smarter with use"
- "After 6 months, you have institutional knowledge that can't be replicated"
- "What would it mean for your team if..."
