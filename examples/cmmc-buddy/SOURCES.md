# Mortgage Underwriting Guidelines — Public Source Documents

All documents below are publicly available from US government agencies and GSEs.
Zero customer data. Pure policy/procedure reference material.

## Primary Sources

### 1. Fannie Mae Selling Guide
- **URL:** https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/
- **Format:** Web (structured HTML), also available as PDF
- **Size:** ~1,200 pages equivalent
- **Key sections for underwriting:**
  - B3: Underwriting Borrowers
  - B4: Underwriting Property
  - B5: Unique Eligibility & Underwriting Considerations
  - B7: Insurance
- **Update frequency:** Multiple times per year (last: Feb 4, 2026)
- **Notes:** Fannie now has their own AI search tool on the site. We can do better with AMP.

### 2. Freddie Mac Seller/Servicer Guide
- **URL:** https://guide.freddiemac.com/
- **PDF:** https://cdn.lhfs.com/lhfscdn/wholesale/download/FreddieMac_TheGuide.pdf
- **Format:** Web + full PDF download
- **Size:** ~1,500+ pages
- **Key sections for underwriting:**
  - Chapter 37: Borrower Qualification
  - Chapter 42: Property Eligibility
  - Chapter 43: Appraisal Requirements
- **Notes:** Often mirrors Fannie but with important differences underwriters need to know

### 3. FHA Single Family Housing Policy Handbook (HUD 4000.1)
- **URL:** https://www.hud.gov/hud-partners/single-family-handbook-4000-1
- **PDF:** https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/4000.1hsgh.pdf
- **Format:** Web + PDF
- **Size:** ~1,000+ pages
- **Key sections:**
  - Section II.A: Origination through Post-Closing/Endorsement (Underwriting)
  - Section II.A.4: Underwriting the Borrower Using the TOTAL Scorecard
  - Section II.A.5: Manual Underwriting of the Borrower
  - Section II.A.6: Property Acceptability Criteria
- **Update frequency:** Regular updates, last Nov 26, 2025
- **Notes:** THE reference for FHA underwriting. Your wife's team uses this constantly.

### 4. VA Lender's Handbook (Pamphlet 26-7)
- **URL:** https://benefits.va.gov/warms/pam26_7.asp
- **Full PDF:** https://www.benefits.va.gov/warms/docs/admin26/m26-07/lender_handbook_va_pamphlet_complete.pdf
- **Chapter 4 (Credit Underwriting):** https://benefits.va.gov/warms/docs/admin26/m26-07/chapter_4_credit_underwriting.pdf
- **Format:** Web (chapter-by-chapter) + PDF
- **Size:** ~500+ pages
- **Key sections:**
  - Chapter 4: Credit Underwriting (the big one)
  - Chapter 6: Appraisals
  - Chapter 7: Closing
- **Notes:** VA has unique rules (no minimum credit score, residual income requirement, etc.)

### 5. USDA Rural Development Handbook (HB-1-3555)
- **URL:** https://www.rd.usda.gov/taxonomy/term/144
- **Full PDF:** https://www.rd.usda.gov/media/file/download/hb-1-3555-consolidated.pdf
- **Format:** Web (chapter-by-chapter) + consolidated PDF
- **Size:** ~400+ pages
- **Key sections:**
  - Chapter 5: Origination and Underwriting
  - Chapter 9: Income Analysis
  - Chapter 10: Credit Analysis
  - Chapter 12: Property and Appraisal Requirements
- **Notes:** Geographic eligibility is a major component (rural area requirement)

## Why This Works as a Demo

An underwriting manager can ask:
- "What's the minimum credit score for an FHA loan?"
- "Can I use rental income from a departing residence on a conventional loan?"
- "What are the reserve requirements for a second home?"
- "What's the difference between Fannie and Freddie DTI requirements?"
- "When can I use a 1099 instead of tax returns for income verification?"

And get answers with **exact guideline citations** — section numbers, page references.
With AMP, corrections and clarifications accumulate over time.
