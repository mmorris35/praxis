# Praxis: Sales Playbook

## The Pitch (30 seconds)

> "Your team spends hours digging through documentation that takes years to master. Praxis turns that documentation into an expert system that gives answers in seconds — and gets smarter every time someone uses it."

## The Problem

Every regulated industry has the same pain:
- **Dense documentation** — hundreds or thousands of pages
- **Expert knowledge gap** — what's written vs. what actually works
- **Tribal knowledge** — walks out the door when people leave
- **Training time** — months to get new hires productive

**Cost:** Senior experts waste time on questions junior staff should handle.

## The Solution

Praxis builds an AI expert system that:
1. **Knows the docs** — ingests and understands your documentation
2. **Knows the practice** — captures what your experts actually do
3. **Learns from use** — every refinement makes it smarter
4. **Preserves knowledge** — institutional memory that doesn't retire

## Differentiation

| Feature | Generic RAG | Praxis |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Reads documentation | ✅ | ✅ |
| Cites sources | ✅ | ✅ |
| Practical guidance | ❌ | ✅ Expert layer |
| Learns from corrections | ❌ | ✅ AMP flywheel |
| Gets smarter over time | ❌ | ✅ Compounds |
| Captures tribal knowledge | ❌ | ✅ Persists |

**The moat:** After 6 months of refinements, you have institutional knowledge that can't be replicated by uploading PDFs.

## Target Markets

### Tier 1: Compliance
- **CMMC** — Defense contractors (300K+ companies need Level 2)
- **HIPAA** — Healthcare (covered entities + business associates)
- **SOC 2** — SaaS companies (every B2B SaaS eventually)
- **ISO 27001** — Enterprise security

### Tier 2: Regulatory
- **FDA** — Medical devices, pharma
- **Financial** — SEC, FINRA, banking regs
- **Environmental** — EPA compliance

### Tier 3: Professional Services
- **Legal** — Case law, contract analysis
- **Medical** — Clinical guidelines
- **Engineering** — Standards, building codes

## Sales Motion

### 1. Lead Generation
- **Content:** "How [COMPANY] cut compliance question resolution from 2 hours to 2 minutes"
- **Webinars:** "Building AI That Actually Knows Your Industry"
- **Referrals:** Happy customers in the same vertical

### 2. Qualification
**BANT:**
- **Budget:** Do they spend on compliance/training tools?
- **Authority:** Who owns documentation/training?
- **Need:** How much time do experts spend answering questions?
- **Timeline:** Audit coming? New regs? Growth?

**Red flags:**
- No documentation (nothing to ingest)
- No experts (no one to capture knowledge from)
- Regulated by "vibes" (no need for accuracy)

### 3. Demo
**Show CMMC-Buddy:**
1. Ask a compliance question
2. Get practical answer with citations
3. Show the "Refine" button
4. Explain: "Every refinement makes it smarter"
5. Show the flywheel diagram

**Key message:** "This is what it looks like after 1 week. Imagine after 6 months."

### 4. Pilot / Proof of Concept
- **Scope:** One narrow domain (one regulation, one process)
- **Duration:** 4 weeks
- **Investment:** $X,XXX (credited to full implementation)
- **Success criteria:** Defined accuracy target

### 5. Close
- SOW for full implementation
- Managed service (preferred) or on-prem license
- Annual contract with growth path

## Pricing Strategy

### Implementation
- **Pilot:** $5K-15K (narrow scope, 4 weeks)
- **Full Implementation:** $25K-75K (depends on domain complexity)

### Ongoing
- **Managed Service:** $2K-5K/month (hosting + support + LLM)
- **On-Prem License:** $50K+ one-time, $10K/year support

### Value Justification
If one senior expert costs $150K/year and spends 10 hours/week answering questions:
- 10 hrs × 50 weeks = 500 hours/year
- 500 hrs × $75/hr = **$37,500/year** in expert time
- Praxis pays for itself if it saves 50% of that time

## Objections & Responses

**"We tried ChatGPT and it hallucinated."**
> "ChatGPT doesn't know your docs or your practice. Praxis only answers from your knowledge base, cites sources, and learns when it's wrong."

**"Our docs are too complex."**
> "Complex docs are exactly why you need this. We've built systems for CMMC (1,955 controls) and mortgage underwriting (6,000+ pages)."

**"What if our expert leaves?"**
> "That's the point. Every refinement captures their knowledge. When they leave, the system keeps it."

**"We can build this ourselves."**
> "You could. It took us 6 months. The real value isn't the code — it's the refinement flywheel that compounds over time. Every month you wait is a month of learning you don't capture."

**"How do we know it's accurate?"**
> "Start with a pilot. Define success criteria. Measure it. If it doesn't hit 85% accuracy in 4 weeks, walk away."
